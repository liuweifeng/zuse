The mobile app can keep more than one environment connection. Each one has a stable identity and label even when its local network address changes.

## Nearby Mac

1. Open **Settings → Computers → Connect to a nearby Mac**.
2. Select the discovered Mac, or wait while Zuse selects it when only one is nearby.
3. Compare the safety phrase on the phone and Mac.
4. Choose **Allow** on the Mac.

The phone stores a device credential and automatically follows new local routes for that same paired Mac. See [Pair a nearby iPhone](/remote/nearby-pairing/) for QR fallback and approval details.

## QR code

On the computer, open **Settings → Remote access**, choose **Create link** under **Connect another device**, then choose **Show QR**. On iPhone, tap the QR button in the inbox header and scan the code. The code works once and expires after five minutes.

## Hosted computer

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Sign in**.
2. Choose **Sign in for remote access**.
3. Sign in with the account used to link the computer.
4. Open **Settings → Computers**.

Account-linked computers appear as **Online**, **Offline**, or **Checking…**. The app automatically connects online computers and includes their chats in the inbox. Local connections continue to work after you sign out.

## Manual connection

Choose **Settings → Computers → Add manually** when discovery cannot be used and you know the environment's host and port. Enter the **Host**, **Port**, and an optional **Token**, then choose **Save connection**. Manual setup is intended for a network route you administer; it does not create hosted reachability on its own.

## Connection states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The live WebSocket is ready. |
| **Connecting** | Zuse is establishing the transport. |
| **Reconnecting** | Zuse is restoring a dropped transport. |
| **Offline** | The app or network is unavailable; cached content remains visible. |
| **Sign in required** | The saved authorization is no longer accepted. |
| **Connection error** | A retryable transport or authorization failure occurred. |

Use the recovery banner's **Retry** action first. Use its QR button to scan a new pairing code only when the credential is missing, expired, or revoked.

## Remove a computer or local data

- In **Settings → Computers**, choose a saved computer, then **Remove Computer**. Its chats disappear from this phone; pair again to reconnect.
- **Settings → Storage → Clear downloaded data** removes cached projects, chats, and messages. Connections and unsent messages remain.
- **Settings → Storage → Clear media cache** removes fetched images and document previews. They download again when needed.
- **Settings → Storage → Reset app** removes account state, saved connections, device keys, cache, and unsent messages from the phone. Your remote account is not deleted.
- Revoking the phone in the computer's **Settings → Remote access** ends its access at the environment.
- **Settings → Account → Delete account** requests permanent deletion of your Zuse account, cloud workspaces and chats, linked-device registrations, and data saved by the app. Files on your own computer are not deleted. It cannot be undone.

<Warning title="Reset removes the outbox">
If you wrote messages while offline, let them send before resetting the app unless you want to discard them.
</Warning>
